What is tab unloading?

Tab unloading means the browser removes a page from memory but keeps the tab open. The tab stays in your tab bar, but its RAM is freed.

Why it helps

Many open tabs use RAM and CPU in the background.
homex can unload inactive tabs so your browser feels faster and uses less memory.

What happens when you click the tab again?

The page reloads. Cookies and logins usually stay

Select unload time

Choose how long an inactive tab can stay open before Homex unloads it. You can pick 15, 30, or 60 minutes. The default is 30 minutes. The tab stays open, but memory is freed

Tip: The less time you select, the more performance your device gets
because RAM is freed sooner. But if the time is too short, tabs reload more often. Use 15 minutes for low RAM, 30 minutes for balance, and 60 minutes for fewer reloads.
